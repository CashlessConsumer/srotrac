# SROTrac — Project Notes

Tracking India's fintech SROs (self-regulatory organisations) and their member rosters.

## Canonical paths

- Register of SROs: `data/sros.csv`
- Member lists: `data/face_members.csv`, `data/uff_members.csv`, `data/srpa_members.csv` (FIDC publishes no roster)
- Queryable DB: `data/srotrac.duckdb` (tables `sros`, `members`)
- Readable output: `docs/members.md`
- Build: `python3 scripts/build.py`
- Raw snapshots: `data/raw/*.html`

## Conventions

- One row per SRO in `sros.csv`; one row per SRO-member pair in the member CSVs.
- `member_type`: `member` or `associate`. Only UFF distinguishes these, and only heuristically — see README caveats. Do not treat it as a filed tier.
- Keep `data/raw/` snapshots with a date record (currently 2026-09-10) so rebuilds are reproducible.
- `docs/members.md` is generated by `scripts/build.py`'s sibling step; regenerate it after any CSV change rather than hand-editing.

## Key facts

- FACE = first RBI-recognised SRO-FT (28 Aug 2024). UFF = second (10 Sep 2026); UFF rebranded from DLAI in Apr 2025.
- 27 organisations appear in more than one SRO (23 in both FACE and UFF; MobiKwik is in all three of FACE/UFF/SRPA) — useful for tracking where positions might align.
- Overlap query: `SELECT member_name FROM members GROUP BY 1 HAVING count(DISTINCT sro)>1;`
