How this is monitored

  • The daily refresh re-fetches the SROs’ own pages, then a drift check (scripts/social_check.py) confirms every ✓-marked account is still linked from its official site. Last drift check 2026-09-25: 23/23 official-site links still live.
  • X, LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram block robots, so follower counts and post contents are not scraped here — links are curated, dated (verified 2026-09-25) and re-verified in the direction platforms can’t block: the SRO’s own website.
  • A handle vanishing from an official site is the cheapest early signal of a rebrand, a takeover or a quietly deleted account — it shows here as a ⚠ flag.
  • Spotted a new or dead handle? It’s a one-row fix in data/social.csv (CC BY 4.0).
