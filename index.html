What is an SRO, in plain words?

RBI can't directly supervise thousands of fintechs, NBFCs and payment companies. So it recognises industry associations as self-regulatory organisations — with a contract: broad membership, independent boards, codes of conduct, monitoring of member compliance, and grievance redress. In return the regulator gets a first line of supervision, and the industry gets a single voice.

For consumers, the SRO is one more place to escalate when a lender or payment company misbehaves — but only if the SRO actually enforces. That is what this tracker watches: rosters, governance, consultations, enforcement, and the gaps in between.
