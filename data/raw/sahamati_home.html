      About AA

      Account Aggregators (AA) use technology to assist you in the simple and secure exchange of your data between financial institutions like banks, insurance agencies, or mutual fund companies. This information cannot be shared without your consent.

      With AA, you can use your financial data to access a vast array of financial services for your personal or business needs.

Real Impact. Across Sectors. Powered by AA

From simplifying customer onboarding to strengthening underwriting, AA is powering a wide range of innovative use cases across lending, investments, insurance, personal finance, and regulatory compliance.

Lending

By providing lenders with instant, consented, and verified financial data, Account Aggregators accelerates credit decisions, reduces operational friction, and expands access to affordable credit for millions, especially first-time and underserved borrowers.

Stockbroking

With seamless, consented access to verified financial data, the Account Aggregator framework is transforming the stockbroking experience. AA enables brokers to complete onboarding faster, assess investor profiles more accurately, and offer personalised investment journeys, helping millions participate in the markets with greater ease, trust, and transparency.

Personal Finance Management (PFM)

Through the Account Aggregator framework, individuals can bring together their financial information from multiple accounts into one consented view. This unified snapshot enables personalised insights, incidental recommendations, and clearer financial decision-making, essentially creating a real-time balance sheet for every individual.

Life Insurance

The Account Aggregator framework is already helping insurers unlock meaningful gains across the value chain. With real-time, consented access to verified financial data, insurance providers are expanding their market reach, driving premium growth, improving operational efficiency, strengthening fraud mitigation, and achieving better persistency outcomes.
